# HerForm – Empowering Women Through Local Opportunities

## About the Project

HerForm is a Local-First Women's Livelihood Opportunity Platform designed to connect skilled women, especially those in rural communities, with customers looking for genuine products and services.

Our goal is to help women turn their existing skills into income opportunities by improving visibility, customer connections, and access to local markets.

## Problem Statement

Many skilled women have difficulty finding customers, marketing their products, receiving fair prices, and accessing flexible earning opportunities.

Limited digital access and concerns about online safety create additional barriers.

## Our Solution

HerForm provides a platform where women can showcase their skills, list products and services, and connect with potential customers.

### Key Features

- Women entrepreneur profiles.
- Product and service listings.
- Search and category filters.
- Customer requirement posting.
- Skill-based opportunity matching.
- Private chat requests with seller approval.
- Blocking and reporting features.
- Order management and tracking.
- Assisted onboarding for women with limited digital access.
- Kannada and English language support (planned).

## Two Ways to Find Opportunities

**Search-first:** Customers discover products and services through listings and contact sellers.

**Request-first:** Customers post requirements, and suitable women can respond with offers.

Both approaches lead to a common order-management process.

## Technology Stack

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Git and GitHub

Backend and database integration are planned for future development.

## Project Status

Frontend prototype under development.

This prototype will initially use demonstration data. Features such as real authentication, private messaging, payments, and database integration will require backend implementation.

## Our Vision

To create a safer, more accessible local digital marketplace where women's skills become meaningful livelihood opportunities.
